**Auto Care** is a modern web-based interface designed to bridge the gap between vehicle owners and service providers in Sri Lanka. The platform focuses on high-quality user experience (UX) to help users find nearby mechanics, emergency help, and spare part providers with ease.

---

## 📱 Interface Overview

The UI design covers a complete end-to-end user journey:

* **🏠 Home Dashboard:** Features a clean interface showing nearby service providers and current vehicle status.
* **🛠️ Service Provider Profiles:** Detailed pages for mechanics and spare part shops including ratings and portfolios.
* **📅 Booking & Payment:** A streamlined flow for scheduling services and making secure online payments.
* **🚨 Emergency Assistance:** A high-priority quick access button for immediate roadside help.
* **👤 User Profile:** Personalized account management for service history and vehicle data.

---

## 🎯 System Objectives

### 🎓 General Objectives
* To enhance the vehicle service experience through high-end automation and AI-driven technology.
* To simplify the marketing process for vehicle service providers using intelligent outreach systems.

### ⚙️ Specific Objectives
* **Vendor Registration:** A robust system for product owners and mechanics to register their businesses.
* **Geolocation Integration:** Utilizing **Google Maps API** for real-time detection of nearby services.
* **AI Marketing:** Implementation of an **AI-powered call bot** to promote products and services automatically.
* **Seamless Booking:** Creating an intuitive search and booking engine for end-users.

---

## 💡 How It Works

The platform operates through a smart 4-step workflow:

1.  **Registration:** Product owners register their businesses and upload detailed inventory/service catalogs.
2.  **Detection:** The system leverages the **Google Maps API** to identify shops and technicians within the user's vicinity.
3.  **AI Outreach:** An **AI bot** automatically initiates calls to market registered products to nearby shops.
4.  **Integration:** Interested shops accept products, which then appear in the public store listings for users to book.
